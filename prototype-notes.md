# Prototype research notes

## Option A — Signal Dashboard

We expect the tester to inspect learning signals and independently decide which learner should be prioritized.

- Watch for whether the Coach can identify priority learners without AI interpretation and how much effort it takes.
- Do not explain what individual signals mean.

## Option B — Explainable Support Queue

We expect the tester to review AI recommendations and supporting evidence before deciding whether to contact or dismiss a learner.

- Watch for whether the Coach checks evidence or trusts the AI ranking immediately.
- Do not explain why the AI assigned a particular priority.

## Option C — AI Support Intervention

We expect the tester to review the proposed intervention and decide whether to edit, approve, postpone or reject it.

- Watch for privacy concerns, hesitation and the level of control the Coach wants.
- Do not explain whether approving the suggestion is the correct action.
